CONCEPT
✦
An equation is a balance
Until now, all the x’s sat politely on one side. In 5x + 2 = 3x + 10, they’re on both. Nothing new is required — you already know the one rule that matters.
⚖️
Key idea: whatever you do to one side, do to the other — including adding or removing x terms. The balance never lies.
VISUAL EXPLAINER
✦
WORKED EXAMPLE
✦
5x + 2 = 3x + 10
STEP 1 Subtract 3x from both sides 2x + 2 = 10
Removing 3x from both pans keeps the balance true — and now every x lives on the left. That’s the whole trick of “both sides” equations.
STEP 2 Subtract 2 from both sides 2x = 8
STEP 3 Divide both sides by 2 x = 4 ✓
NOTE — PINNED FROM CHAT
Why gather the x terms first?
Removing an x term from both sides keeps the equation true and collects the unknowns together — after that, it’s a one-step solve. Subtracting the smaller x term avoids negatives.

✦ CONCEPT · EXPLAINED BY PETROS
An equation is a balance
The equals sign is a promise: both sides are the same number, even if they look different. Solving an equation means keeping that promise while you simplify — every move you make on the left, you make on the right, until x stands alone.
When x appears on both sides, you first move all the x terms to one side (subtract the smaller one from both sides), then solve as usual. Two moves, always the same two moves.
Both sides of the = are equal. Keep them equal.
If you take 3x away from the left, take 3x away from the right too. Do that until only x is left on one side. That number is your answer.
Imagine an old market scale, perfectly level. The left pan holds 5 mystery parcels and 2 kg of weights; the right holds 3 parcels and 10 kg. Level pans mean equal weight.
Lift 3 parcels off each pan — still level. Take 2 kg off each — still level. Now 2 parcels balance 8 kg, so each parcel weighs 4 kg. You just solved 5x + 2 = 3x + 10.
Two phone plans: Plan A charges ₹50 up front plus ₹20 per GB. Plan B charges ₹110 up front plus ₹10 per GB. When do they cost the same?
That question is the equation 20x + 50 = 10x + 110. Gather the x’s: 10x = 60, so x = 6 GB. Below 6 GB, pick A; above, pick B.
Because equality is preserved by any function applied to both sides: if a = b, then f(a) = f(b). Adding 3, subtracting 2x, dividing by 2 — each is a function. The one caution: dividing by something that could be zero, which is why we never divide by an expression containing x without checking.
This idea scales all the way up — it’s the same principle you’ll use in calculus and beyond.

The limit of a function
We say the limit of f(x) as x approaches a equals L if f(x) can be made arbitrarily close to L by taking x sufficiently close to a — from either side, but not necessarily at a itself.
limx→a f(x) = L
The two-sided limit exists only when the left-hand and right-hand limits agree. Where they disagree, the limit does not exist — a jump discontinuity.
