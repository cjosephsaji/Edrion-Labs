# First-time learner test — ready to run

No human participants were available in this implementation session. No human results are claimed. Automated browser recordings are separate evidence.

Recruit at least five learners who have not used this prototype. Use a fresh browser tab for each learner; select AI Module 3 without opening the source PDF. Do not explain the navigation or the rules beforehand. Allow roughly 10–15 minutes per learner and obtain permission before recording.

Give these tasks, one at a time:

1. Start learning minimax. Say what you think you are learning and what you would do next.
2. Explain MAX in your own words, then answer its visible question.
3. Explain MIN in your own words. Choose 8 once at MIN so you can see how Petros responds to a misunderstanding.
4. Recover using Petros's explanation and solve the fresh example.
5. Ask Petros why MIN B would choose 2 instead of 9. Resume the same task.
6. Solve the unfamiliar final tree and explain why its tempting largest leaf is not necessarily the best move.

Observe before prompting. Record the learner's words and actions, not inferred understanding. If they need facilitator help, record exactly what was said. Afterwards ask which explanation helped, what was confusing, and whether they knew how to continue.
