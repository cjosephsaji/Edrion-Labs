Read the worked explanation
First divide both sides of 3(x + 2) = 18 by 3, giving x + 2 = 6. Then subtract 2 from both sides, giving x = 4. This worked example is only for comparing presentation patterns.

What is claim, evidence and reasoning?
Claim: the point you want to make. Evidence: a detail from a source that supports it. Reasoning: your explanation of why that detail supports your point. For production, source texts and feedback must be authorized and grounded.
